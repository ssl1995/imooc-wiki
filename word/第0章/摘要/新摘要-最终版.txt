古树名木是自然生态与历史文化的重要载体，其保护与管理对生物多样性维护和文化遗产传承具有重要意义。当前，园林局与林业管理部门在古树名木的建设、检索与管理中，仍普遍依赖人工巡查与文字记录方式，存在图文信息割裂、数据更新滞后、品种检索效率低等问题。由于部分古树品种形态相似，且部分记录信息仅以文字或非结构化图像形式保存，传统单模态检索方法难以满足实际精细化管理需求。因此，构建一个融合文本图像的多模态检索系统，通过跨模态技术实现模态间信息互通，成为提升古树名木保护与管理高效化的关键突破口。本文提出一种基于深度哈希学习的跨模态检索模型CHNATR（Cross-modal Hashing Network for Ancient Tree Retrieval），实现以文搜图、以图搜文的双向检索能力。本文主要工作如下：
（1）针对古树名木跨模态检索中样本类别不平衡与复杂背景干扰等问题，提出CHNATR模型。首先，设计基于Triplet Loss和Contrastive Loss的联合损失函数进行度量学习优化，并采用难例挖掘策略缓解类别不平衡问题。其次，构建哈希层（FC-Tanh-Sign）和量化损失（Quantization Loss）模块，将连续特征映射为128位二进制哈希码，降低存储成本并加速检索。最后，设计跨模态注意力融合机制，联合文本和图像信息构建统一语义空间，增强模型对古树关键特征的判别能力。
（2）为系统评估模型性能，构建多维度实验验证框架。首先，将CHNATR与DCMH、PRDH等四个跨模态哈希基线模型进行性能对比，验证架构优越性。其次，在MSVD（提取关键帧作为图像-文本对）、iNaturalist 2018等数据集上进行mAP@K、Recall@K和Precision@K指标对比，验证模型有效性。最后，进行消融实验验证哈希层、注意力融合机制及联合损失函数等各模块的贡献。实验结果表明，CHNATR在检索速度和准确率上均优于对比方法。
（3）针对现有系统缺少专用古树名木检索工具的问题，设计实现基于B/S架构的古树名木检索系统。系统采用Vue和SpringBoot前后端分离架构，集成CHNATR模型支持文本-图像双向检索。目前系统已完成开发和测试，运行稳定。
关键词: 古树名木检索，深度哈希学习，跨模态检索，CHNATR模型

Research and Construction of Ancient and Famous Trees Retrieval System Based on Text-Image Multimodal Fusion
Master Candidate: Song Sheng Lin
(Software Engineering)
Directed by Tian Xuan

Abstract
Ancient and famous trees are important carriers of natural ecology and historical culture, and their protection and management are of great significance for biodiversity conservation and cultural heritage inheritance. At present, the construction, retrieval and management of ancient and famous trees by landscape bureaus and forestry management departments still generally rely on manual inspection and written records, resulting in problems such as separation of image and text information, delayed data update, and low efficiency of species retrieval. Due to the similar morphology of some ancient tree species and the fact that part of the recorded information is only stored in the form of text or unstructured images, traditional unimodal retrieval methods can hardly meet the needs of practical refined management. Therefore, constructing a multimodal retrieval system fusing text and image information, and realizing information exchange between modalities through cross-modal technology, has become a key breakthrough to improve the efficiency of ancient and famous tree protection and management. This study proposes a cross-modal retrieval model CHNATR (Cross-modal Hashing Network for Ancient Tree Retrieval) based on deep hashing learning, which realizes bidirectional retrieval capabilities of text-to-image and image-to-text. The main contributions of this paper are as follows:

(1) To address the problems of sample class imbalance and complex background interference in cross-modal retrieval of ancient trees, the CHNATR model is proposed. Firstly, a joint loss function based on Triplet Loss and Contrastive Loss is designed for metric learning optimization, and hard negative mining strategy is adopted to alleviate the class imbalance problem. Secondly, a hash layer (FC-Tanh-Sign) and quantization loss module are constructed to map continuous features into 128-bit binary hash codes, reducing storage costs and accelerating retrieval. Finally, a cross-modal attention fusion mechanism is designed to construct a unified semantic space by combining text and image information, enhancing the model's discriminative ability for key features of ancient trees.

(2) To systematically evaluate the model performance, a multi-dimensional experimental verification framework is constructed. Firstly, CHNATR is compared with four cross-modal hashing baseline models including DCMH and PRDH to verify the superiority of the architecture. Secondly, comparisons on mAP@K, Recall@K and Precision@K metrics are conducted on datasets such as MSVD (extracting key frames as image-text pairs) and iNaturalist 2018 to verify the model effectiveness. Finally, ablation experiments are carried out to verify the contributions of the hash layer, attention fusion mechanism and joint loss function. Experimental results demonstrate that CHNATR outperforms comparison methods in retrieval speed and accuracy.

(3) To solve the lack of dedicated retrieval tools for ancient and famous trees, a B/S architecture-based ancient tree retrieval system is designed and implemented. The system adopts a front-end and back-end separated architecture using Vue and SpringBoot, and integrates the CHNATR model to support bidirectional text-image retrieval. At present, the system has completed development and testing, and runs stably.

Key Words: Ancient and Famous Tree Retrieval; Deep Hashing Learning; Cross-modal Retrieval; CHNATR Model
