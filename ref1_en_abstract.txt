Abstract
In the field of food sensory research, consumer review texts carry a wealth of sensory evaluation information. In-depth analysis of these texts can help us accurately grasp the sensory characteristics of food and explore consumer preferences and consumption experiences. Existing food sensory research mainly relies on offline experiments and manual analysis, which is not only time-consuming and labor-intensive, but also limited in data scale, and may be influenced by the subjective tendencies of analysts. Based on the above issues, to improve the efficiency and scientificity of food sensory research, this study proposes a fine-grained sensory analysis model FGSAM-SI (Fine-Grained Sensory Analysis Model with Sensory Intensity) based on aspect-level opinion word extraction, and designs and implements a food sensory analysis system oriented to tasting texts based on this model, providing food-related information management and fine-grained sensory analysis functions. The main work of this paper is as follows:
(1) We propose a fine-grained sensory analysis model FGSAM-SI, which aims to accurately extract sensory words and their corresponding intensities in food review texts based on deep learning technology, and realize the fine-grained analysis of consumers' sensory experiences of food. Firstly, we design an intensity attention mechanism in the model to enhance the representation ability of sensory intensity words in the input sequence. Secondly, to further associate intensity words with corresponding sensory words, we design an intensity syntax tree to learn the syntactic relationships in food review texts, so as to more accurately capture the connection between sensory words and intensity words, and thereby improve the overall sensory analysis effect of various aspects of food.
(2) To validate the performance of the FGSAM-SI model, we constructed a proprietary food sensory analysis dataset based on the results of the Projective Mapping & Flash Profile (PM&FP) sensory experiment of the School of Biological Sciences at Beijing Forestry University, and validated the model performance based on this dataset. The experimental results show that increasing the intensity attention mechanism and the intensity syntax tree respectively improve the precision of sensory word and sensory intensity extraction by 3.73% and 5.1%, showing significant advantages in the task of fine-grained food sensory analysis.
(3) Based on the existing food flavor compound information platform, we optimize and iterate it, and design and implement a food sensory analysis system oriented to review texts, providing intelligent and informatized support for food sensory research. This system can collect Internet review data of specified foods and conduct fine-grained food sensory analysis and visualization in combination with the FGSAM-SI model. The backend of the system uses SpringBoot and Flask frameworks, and the frontend is based on the Vue framework, to ensure the system runs stably and efficiently and meets the needs of food sensory researchers.
Key Words: Review Text, Sensory Analysis System, Opinion Word Extraction, Attention Mechanism, Syntax Tree
